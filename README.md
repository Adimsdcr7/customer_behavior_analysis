# customer_behavior_analysis

This project analyzes customer purchase data to understand shopping patterns, spending behavior, and factors that influence sales and loyalty.

## Overview
The goal of this project is to explore how customers shop across different product categories and identify what drives higher spending. The analysis focuses on customer demographics, discounts, subscriptions, and product performance.

## Problem Statement
Retail businesses collect a lot of customer data, but it’s often unclear:
- Which customers generate the most revenue
- Whether discounts are actually helping sales
- If subscriptions change buying behavior
- Which products and categories perform best

This project answers these questions using data.

## Dataset
- 3,900 customer purchase records
- 18 features per transaction
- 4 product categories: Clothing, Accessories, Footwear, Outerwear
- Includes age, gender, ratings, discounts, shipping type, and subscription status

## Tools Used
- Python (data cleaning and analysis)
- SQL (querying and segmentation)
- Power BI (dashboard and visualization)

## What I Did
- Cleaned and prepared raw customer data
- Handled missing values and standardized columns
- Created customer groups based on age and loyalty
- Analyzed revenue and sales across different segments
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard

## Key Insights
- Most customers are non-subscribers, yet they contribute the majority of revenue
- Male customers spend more overall compared to female customers
- Several products rely heavily on discounts
- Young adults generate the highest revenue
- A large portion of customers are already loyal

## Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard shows:
- Total customers, average purchase value, and ratings
- Revenue and sales by category and age group
- Filters for gender, subscription status, product category, and shipping type
